## Thesis Evolution

### How the Thesis Was Built

PhyCap's investment thesis originates from clinical practice, not from market analysis layered onto healthcare from the outside. The three focus areas reflect problems the GP team encounters, has encountered, or has deep exposure to through their combined 168+ years of clinical work:

**Clinical Care Delivery Workflow Optimization:** Physicians experience workflow inefficiency daily. EHR friction, care coordination gaps, administrative burden, and clinical decision support failures are not abstract market categories for PhyCap's GPs. They are Tuesday afternoon problems. This thesis area targets companies building tools that make clinical work function better, evaluated by the people who do that work.

**SaMD (FDA Class I/II):** Software as a Medical Device is a regulatory category that requires both clinical understanding (what the software does in a care context) and regulatory fluency (how it gets cleared). PhyCap's physician GPs bring the clinical side. The Class I/II focus keeps the regulatory pathway manageable for seed-stage companies and avoids the longer timelines and higher capital requirements of Class III devices.

**Women's Health and Health Inequity:** This thesis area reflects both a market opportunity (Women's Health is historically underfunded relative to market size) and a values alignment within the GP team. The Women's Health Nexus event (200+ attendees, Congressional Country Club, November 2025) is evidence that PhyCap is not just investing in this space but actively building the ecosystem around it.

### Thesis Boundaries

The exclusion of life sciences and pharmaceuticals is deliberate, not a limitation. These categories require different capital structures (longer timelines, higher check sizes, different regulatory expertise) and different GP backgrounds (bench science, drug development) than PhyCap's team offers. The exclusion sharpens the thesis and prevents mission drift.

## Portfolio Construction Framework

### Deployment Strategy for Fund I

| Parameter | Target | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Total investments | 15-25 companies | $10M fund with $150K-$500K checks. Allows diversification while maintaining meaningful ownership. |
| Thesis allocation | Roughly balanced across three areas | Avoids over-concentration in one thesis area while building pattern recognition across all three. |
| Reserve strategy | 30-40% of fund reserved for follow-on | Seed-stage portfolio construction requires follow-on capacity. Reserves protect ownership in winners. |
| Geographic focus | US-based companies, no geographic restriction within US | Tulsa HQ provides Midwest access, but thesis areas produce companies nationally. |
| Stage discipline | First institutional check at Seed, follow-on at Series A | Maintaining stage discipline prevents scope creep into later rounds where PhyCap's check size is less impactful. |

### Diligence Value-Add

The GP team's clinical expertise creates diligence advantages that traditional healthcare VCs and generalist clinician communities cannot replicate:

1. **Specialty-matched evaluation:** A SaMD for radiology workflows is evaluated by a radiologist GP. A Women's Health diagnostic is evaluated by an OB/GYN GP. The diligence is conducted by a physician whose daily practice maps to the product's use case.

2. **Clinical workflow validation:** GPs can assess whether a product's claimed workflow improvement is real, marginal, or dependent on conditions that do not exist in typical practice settings. This is faster and more accurate than survey-based clinical validation.

3. **Adoption prediction:** Physicians adopt tools based on factors that non-clinicians often misunderstand (EHR integration friction, liability concerns, reimbursement alignment, peer behavior). PhyCap's GPs can assess adoption likelihood with a specificity that market research cannot match.

## Innovation Circle Strategy

The Innovation Circle is PhyCap's physician community beyond the GP team. It serves three functions:

1. **Deal flow:** Innovation Circle physicians encounter potential investment opportunities in their clinical practice. A structured process for surfacing these to the GP team creates a deal flow channel that scales with membership.

2. **Diligence support:** When a deal requires subspecialty expertise beyond the GP team, Innovation Circle physicians provide supplementary clinical assessment. This extends PhyCap's effective clinical coverage without expanding the GP team.

3. **LP recruitment:** Innovation Circle engagement is a top-of-funnel LP recruitment mechanism. Physicians who participate in diligence conversations, attend events, and see deal flow are more likely to invest as LPs than physicians who encounter PhyCap cold.

**Growth targets:**
- Current: Baseline membership (exact count to be documented)
- End of 2026: 50+ active members
- End of 2027: 100+ active members
- End of 2028: 200+ active members with structured engagement tiers
