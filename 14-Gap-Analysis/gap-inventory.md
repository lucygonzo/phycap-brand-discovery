# Gap Inventory

## Current State > Desired State Gaps

Each gap includes: what exists now, what should exist, severity (Critical / High / Medium / Low), and action direction.

---

### Gap 1: MHCDS Credential Is Invisible
**Current State**: The MHCDS designation appears after Paul Slosar's and Robin Noble's names on the team page. No explanation of the credential, its curriculum, or its relevance to PhyCap's investment thesis exists anywhere on the website, LinkedIn, or Resources content.

**Desired State**: A dedicated content pillar explaining what the Dartmouth MHCDS is, how it trains physicians in healthcare systems architecture, and why graduates of this program evaluate deals differently than MBAs or physicians without systems training. MHCDS should be referenced in the positioning statement, the diligence process description, and GP profile content.

**Severity**: Critical. This is PhyCap's single most defensible differentiator and it is doing zero work for the brand.

**Action Direction**: Create an MHCDS explainer article for the Resources page. Incorporate MHCDS into the positioning statement. Reference the credential in Paul's and Robin's GP content profiles. Add a brief explanation to the team page bios.

---

### Gap 2: Conversion Pages Not Publicly Accessible
**Current State**: LEARN, INVEST, PITCH, and DISCLOSURES pages return errors or are not publicly accessible during research. Navigation links to these pages exist but lead to dead ends for public visitors.

**Desired State**: Either these pages are publicly accessible with appropriate gating (accredited investor verification for INVEST, application form for PITCH), or the navigation clearly indicates that these sections require authentication, with a clear path to obtain access.

**Severity**: Critical. Two primary conversion paths (LP investment, founder pitch submission) dead-end at these pages.

**Action Direction**: Clarify with Kate whether pages are gated, broken, or in development. If gated, add messaging on the navigation link or a landing page that explains how to access. If broken, fix. If in development, remove from navigation until live.

### Gap 3: Contact Email Routes Through FortySix Capital

**Desired State**: info@phycapfund.com as the primary contact, with the FortySix Capital co-management relationship explained in a brief statement on the website.

**Action Direction**: Establish @phycapfund.com email. Update contact page. Add co-management disclosure to website footer or About section. See 02-Company/company-overview.md for detailed recommendation.

### Gap 5: Positioning Statement Is Vague and Interchangeable
**Current State**: "Physicians Capital Fund is more than just a Venture Capital Fund: it's a transformative initiative created by and for physicians and healthcare professionals, dedicated to shaping the future of healthcare."

**Desired State**: A positioning statement that names PhyCap's specific differentiators (MHCDS GPs, 13+ specialty diligence team, the moment of truth investment frame) and makes a claim no competitor can make.

**Severity**: High. The current statement could be used by any healthcare fund without modification.

**Action Direction**: Adopt replacement positioning statement from 01-Identity/positioning-statement.md. Retire "transformative initiative," "shaping the future," and "more than just."

---

### Gap 6: Diligence Process Is Not Visible
**Current State**: "Investing with Clinical Precision" is the tagline. The website does not explain what the diligence process actually involves, which GPs review deals, what clinical questions they ask, or how the multi-specialty review works.

**Desired State**: A "How We Evaluate" section or page that shows the diligence process step by step, maps GP specialties to the clinical questions they ask, and demonstrates why "Clinical Precision" is a process description, not just marketing language.

**Severity**: High. The tagline promises something the website does not show. This gap undermines credibility with both LPs (who want to understand how their capital is deployed) and founders (who want to know what to expect).

**Action Direction**: Create diligence process content. See 05-Services/diligence-process.md for detailed framework.

---

### Gap 7: CTA Hierarchy Is Unclear
**Current State**: Multiple CTAs compete across pages: "Schedule time with us," "JOIN NOW," "INVEST WITH US," "SUBSCRIBE," "Book a 1:1 Call," "CONTACT." No audience-specific CTA hierarchy guides physicians to one path and founders to another.

**Desired State**: One primary CTA per audience per page. Physician LP pages lead to "Join the Innovation Circle" (community entry) or "Book an LP Conversation" (qualified). Founder pages lead to "Submit Your Pitch." General pages default to "Schedule time with us."

**Severity**: Medium. The current state creates friction but does not completely block conversion.

**Action Direction**: Map CTA hierarchy per page per audience. Redesign CTA buttons with visual hierarchy (primary color for primary CTA, secondary treatment for supporting CTAs).

### Gap 8: No Event Brand System

**Severity**: Medium. The event worked. The brand capital from it is not being compounded.

**Action Direction**: Design event brand system. Create Women's Health Nexus landing page. Develop post-event content package. Position as annual franchise in 2026 planning.
