# Perception Scores

## Perception Banner

**PhyCap presents as a credible, physician-led fund with strong visual consistency and a defensible thesis, but significant gaps in portfolio credibility, funnel integrity, and verbal differentiation prevent the brand from reaching the authority level the underlying product deserves.**

---

## Dimension Scores

Scoring: 1-10 scale. Green (6-10): strength to build on. Amber (4-5): functional but needs improvement. Red (1-3): critical gap requiring immediate attention.

### 1. Brand Clarity
**Score: 5 (Amber)**

The investment thesis is clear (three pillars, defined check size, explicit exclusions). The tagline is memorable and service-marked. However, the positioning statement is vague and interchangeable with any healthcare fund. The brand name usage is inconsistent (PhyCap vs. Physicians Capital Fund vs. PhyCap Fund). The FortySix Capital relationship is unexplained. The MHCDS credential, the fund's most defensible differentiator, is invisible. The brand knows what it is but has not yet articulated why it is the only one.

**What would move this to green**: Replace the positioning statement (see 01-Identity/positioning-statement.md). Establish naming conventions. Explain the FortySix Capital relationship publicly. Build the MHCDS narrative into core brand assets.

### 2. Digital Presence
**Score: 5 (Amber)**

The website is professionally designed with consistent visual identity. LinkedIn is active (1,132 followers, multiple posts per week). However, multiple navigation pages (LEARN, INVEST, PITCH, DISCLOSURES) were not publicly accessible during research. The contact email routes through @46.capital. No portfolio showcase exists. The Resources page is functional but lacks visual engagement (no pull quotes, data visualizations, or social share graphics). The site feels like a well-designed MVP rather than a mature fund's digital home.

**What would move this to green**: Resolve page accessibility issues. Establish @phycapfund.com as primary contact. Add portfolio showcase. Redesign Resources page with visual content system. Add "As Seen In" media section.

### 3. Content Quality
**Score: 7 (Green)**

The Resources page has 8+ articles covering relevant topics (2026 sector predictions, Women's Health Nexus recap, AI in healthcare, wearables adoption, healthcare economics). Tone is appropriate: thought leadership pitched to physician audience without excessive jargon. Webinar series is active with named speakers. Podcast appearances on credible outlets (Becker's, The Imaging Wire, The Surgeon's Record). Content exists and is good. The gap is in distribution and GP amplification, not quality.

**What would move this higher**: Coordinated GP content system (Page 19). Visual content templates (carousel graphics, stat cards, pull quote designs). Cross-linking between articles. SEO optimization for target keywords.

### 4. Market Awareness
**Score: 4 (Amber)**

PhyCap is recognized in the physician-led VC niche (RyOrtho coverage, Becker's podcast, 1,132 LinkedIn followers). The Women's Health Nexus event drew 200+ attendees. However, the fund is not yet a recognized name in the broader healthcare VC conversation. SEO presence for competitive terms ("healthcare VC," "physician investor," "SaMD investment") is likely minimal. Brand recall outside the existing network is untested.

**What would move this to green**: PR push around the MHCDS differentiator. Annual Women's Health Nexus franchise. GP personal brand content at full cadence. Targeted media placements in healthcare trade publications beyond ortho-focused outlets.

### 5. Portfolio Credibility
**Score: 2 (Red)**

BioReact is the only publicly confirmed portfolio company. No case studies, logos, founder testimonials, or outcome data appear on the website. No "As Seen In" or media logo section. The portfolio page is effectively empty. For a fund actively raising, this is the most critical credibility gap. Every LP conversation that reaches "show me what you have invested in" encounters a wall.

**What would move this to amber**: Add BioReact showcase with whatever detail is permissible. Create an anonymized case study template. Add founder testimonial (even one). Display media logos. If additional portfolio companies exist under NDA, prepare for their public announcement.

### 6. Visual Identity Completeness
**Score: 6 (Green)**

The confirmed palette (Forest #122620, Gold #D3B184, Blue Black #070808, Dark Slate #1F2D35, Cream #F5F2F0, White #FFFFFF, Black #000000) is applied consistently. Photography style (desaturated B&W clinical imagery) is consistent. Logo usage is uniform across pages. However, only two CDN-hosted PNG logo files are confirmed. No SVG versions, high-res PNGs, icon-only mark, stacked lockup, or favicon are documented. Typography has not been formally confirmed (approximate identifications only). The visual system is consistent but incomplete.

**What would move this higher**: Confirm exact font names. Produce full logo asset kit. Document photo treatment as replicable preset (see 03-Visual/photo-treatment-preset.md). Build AI graphics master prompt.

### 7. Verbal Consistency
**Score: 4 (Amber)**

The tagline ("Investing with Clinical Precision") is used consistently. The mission statement is repeated. However, the positioning statement is vague and the brand alternates between empowerment language ("empower medical professionals"), community language ("connected ecosystem"), and precision language ("clinical precision") without a clear hierarchy. Several phrases identified for retirement ("more than just a Venture Capital Fund," "transformative initiative," "shaping the future of healthcare") still appear prominently. The verbal identity has a strong anchor (the tagline) but inconsistent supporting structure.

**What would move this to green**: Adopt the messaging hierarchy in 01-Identity/messaging-hierarchy.md. Retire identified phrases. Operationalize the "moment of truth" narrative frame across all content.

### 8. Funnel Integrity
**Score: 3 (Red)**

The physician LP path breaks at the INVEST page (not publicly accessible during research). The founder path breaks at the PITCH page (not publicly accessible during research). Multiple CTAs compete for attention without clear audience-specific hierarchy. The community signup form captures Medical Specialty (good for segmentation) but has no visible follow-up sequence documented. The contact email routes through @46.capital. The conversion architecture from awareness through investment/pitch has multiple friction points and at least two dead-ends.

**What would move this to amber**: Resolve LEARN/INVEST/PITCH page accessibility. Establish clear CTA hierarchy per audience per page. Build documented follow-up sequences for form submissions. Move contact email to @phycapfund.com.
