## Phase 1: Pre-Kate Meeting (Before Friday April 3)

**Objective:** Surface every unresolved brand system decision so Kate can approve, adjust, or reject in a single working session. No decision should require a follow-up meeting.

### 1.1 Email Domain Confusion + Establish @phycapfund.com as Primary

**Problem:** Current contact email (info@46.capital) creates confusion about whether the fund is PhyCap or FortySix Capital. LPs and founders reaching out encounter a domain that does not match the brand name they were pitched.

**Solution direction:** Register and configure @phycapfund.com. Redirect 46.capital inbound to the new domain during transition. Update all public-facing touchpoints (website footer, pitch deck, LinkedIn company page, GP email signatures) within 48 hours of Kate approval.

**Decision needed from Kate:** Confirm @phycapfund.com as primary. Decide whether @46.capital remains active as a redirect or is deprecated entirely.

### 1.2 Typography Unconfirmed + Inspect Live Site CSS for Exact Font Stack

**Problem:** The brand vault cannot document typography guidance without knowing what fonts the live site currently uses. Any new templates or content assets risk visual inconsistency if built on assumptions.

**Solution direction:** Inspect phycapfund.com CSS to extract the exact font-family declarations for headings, body, and UI elements. Cross-reference against the visual tone of Insider Precision (likely a clean serif or refined sans-serif). Present findings to Kate with a recommendation to either confirm current fonts or propose alternatives.

**Decision needed from Kate:** Approve current typography or authorize a type refresh aligned with the confirmed palette.

### 1.3 Brand Archetype Pending + Confirm Insider Precision

**Problem:** "Insider Precision" has been proposed as the brand archetype but has not been formally ratified. Every verbal identity decision downstream (tone, vocabulary, tagline extensions) depends on this anchor.

**Solution direction:** Present the Insider Precision framework to Kate with examples of how it governs tone, content, and visual decisions. Show the contrast with rejected alternatives (Authority/Prestige, Disruptor, etc.). Get a firm yes or a redirect.

**Decision needed from Kate:** Confirm Insider Precision as the governing brand archetype, or specify adjustments.

### 1.4 FortySix Capital Visibility Undefined + Decide Co-Brand Presentation

**Problem:** The relationship between PhyCap (the fund brand) and FortySix Capital (the management company or parent entity) is unclear in public-facing materials. LPs may encounter both names without understanding the hierarchy. The 46.capital domain compounds this.

**Solution direction:** Define one of three models: (a) FortySix Capital is invisible to external audiences, (b) FortySix Capital appears as a small "managed by" or "a FortySix Capital fund" notation, or (c) FortySix Capital has its own brand presence alongside PhyCap. Each model has different implications for the website, pitch deck, and legal disclosures.

**Decision needed from Kate:** Select the co-brand visibility model.

### 1.5 Logo Assets Incomplete + Commission Full Asset Kit

**Problem:** Only two logo files are confirmed (logo-dark-120.png for light backgrounds, logo-120.png for dark backgrounds). Both are 120px PNGs, which are insufficient for print, high-resolution digital, favicon, social media, and merchandise applications.

**Solution direction:** Commission or extract from the design source file a complete logo kit: SVG (scalable), high-resolution PNG (minimum 1200px), icon-only mark (no wordmark), stacked lockup (mark above wordmark), horizontal lockup, and favicon (16x16, 32x32, 192x192). All variants in Forest on Cream, Cream on Forest, Gold on Forest, and Black on White.

**Decision needed from Kate:** Confirm the logo mark is final (no redesign anticipated) and approve asset kit production.
