## 4. No Em Dashes in Any PhyCap Content

**Decision date:** Pre-vault (confirmed brand rule)
**Decision maker:** Brand Lead

**Rule:** Em dashes (the long dash character) are prohibited in all PhyCap-branded content. This includes LinkedIn posts, website copy, pitch decks, LP communications, email signatures, and internal documents.

**Alternatives:**
- Use a period and start a new sentence
- Use a colon to introduce a related idea
- Use parentheses for asides
- Restructure the sentence to eliminate the need for an interruption

**Rationale:** Em dashes create a tone of breathless urgency or casual interruption that conflicts with the Insider Precision archetype. PhyCap's voice is measured, deliberate, and structurally clean. Every sentence should feel intentional.

**Decision date:** Pre-vault (confirmed during brand discovery)
**Decision maker:** Kate / GP team

**Rule:** "Investing with Clinical Precision" is a service-marked phrase. It is the fund's primary tagline and positioning statement. This phrase is not up for replacement, revision, or A/B testing. It may be extended (e.g., used as a headline followed by supporting copy) but the core phrase remains fixed.

**Usage guidance:**
- Always appears with the service mark symbol on first use in formal materials: Investing with Clinical Precision(SM)
- On LinkedIn and social media, the SM notation is optional but the phrase should be used verbatim
- Do not paraphrase as "clinical precision investing" or "precision clinical investment" or similar rearrangements
- The phrase anchors the Insider Precision archetype: it tells the market that this fund's precision comes from clinical experience, not financial engineering

## 6. Investment Thesis: Three Pillars Confirmed

**Decision date:** Pre-vault (confirmed during brand discovery)
**Decision maker:** GP team

**The three pillars:**

1. **Clinical Care Delivery Workflow Optimization:** Technology that improves the systems and processes through which care is actually delivered. Not back-office or billing optimization, but the clinical workflow itself.

2. **Software as a Medical Device (SaMD), FDA Class I/II:** Software that qualifies as a medical device under FDA classification, focused on Class I (low risk) and Class II (moderate risk). This keeps the fund in the regulatory sweet spot: meaningful clinical impact without the timeline and capital demands of Class III devices.

3. **Women's Health and Health Inequity:** Investing in populations and conditions that have been systematically under-resourced by traditional healthcare venture capital. Women's health is the anchor, with broader health inequity as the frame.

**Unifying concept:** The fund invests at "the moment of truth in medicine." This is the moment care is delivered, the device present at that moment, the patient most underserved at that moment. All three pillars converge on this point.

**Rationale:** These pillars are not arbitrary verticals. They reflect the clinical experience of 10 GPs across 13+ specialties and 168+ years of practice. Each pillar maps to a pattern the GPs have seen repeatedly: workflow friction that technology could resolve, device opportunities in the regulatory middle ground, and patient populations where the gap between clinical need and available capital is widest.

## 7. Typography: Marcellus + Roboto

**Decision date:** March 31, 2026 (confirmed via live site inspection)
**Decision maker:** Existing site design (confirmed by Lucy)

**Confirmed fonts:**

| Role | Font | Source | Weight(s) |
|------|------|--------|-----------|
| Headlines | Marcellus | Google Fonts (free, Apache 2.0) | Regular (single weight) |
| Body / Navigation / Labels | Roboto | Google Fonts (free, Apache 2.0) | Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700) |

**Usage rules:**
- Marcellus for all hero headlines, section titles, stat numbers, and display text
- Roboto for body copy, subheadings, navigation, CTAs, eyebrow labels (all caps with tracking), captions
- No third typeface. The Marcellus/Roboto pairing is complete.
- Fallbacks: Georgia (Marcellus fallback), Arial (Roboto fallback)

**Rationale:** Marcellus carries the institutional authority PhyCap needs (classical Roman proportions, high contrast) while Roboto provides the clinical readability (clean, geometric, widely familiar). Both are free Google Fonts with no licensing restrictions, simplifying deployment across web, email, templates, presentations, and GP content.
