## Profile Photo Treatment: PhyCap Photo Preset

All GP profile photos should be processed through a consistent treatment that creates visual cohesion across the four profiles without making them look artificially identical.

### PhyCap Photo Treatment Specifications

| Parameter | Setting |
|-----------|---------|
| Saturation | Reduced 20 to 30% from original (desaturated, not grayscale) |
| Color temperature | Shifted 10 to 15% cooler (away from warm skin tones toward neutral) |
| Shadow tint | Forest green (#122620) at 8 to 12% opacity in shadow regions |
| Contrast | Slightly increased (+5 to 10%) to maintain crispness after desaturation |
| Background | Simplified or replaced with neutral tone if original is distracting |
| Crop | Head and shoulders, centered, with approximately 20% space above the head |

### Why This Treatment Works

- **Desaturation** prevents the photos from competing with the brand palette. LinkedIn profiles with highly saturated, color-rich photos create visual noise alongside PhyCap's restrained Forest + Gold palette.
- **Cool shift** moves the photos toward the cool end of the spectrum, aligning with Forest and Dark Slate rather than warm reds and oranges.
- **Forest shadow tint** is the signature element. It subtly connects each GP's photo to the brand palette without being obvious. The effect should be felt, not seen.
- **Consistency** across four photos creates a visual signal that these physicians belong to the same fund, even when their profiles are viewed independently.

### Photo Source Requirements

- Professional headshot (not a conference photo, not a selfie, not a clinical photo)
- Neutral expression or slight smile (approachable authority)
- Business or business-casual attire (no white coats, no scrubs, no surgical caps)
- If new photos are needed, shoot against a neutral background and apply the treatment in post-production

## Gaps in This Section

- **Banner production.** Design directions are described but no designer is assigned to produce the actual banner files. Specs need to be converted into design briefs with asset requirements.
- **Photo status.** It is unknown whether all four GPs have professional headshots suitable for the PhyCap treatment. New photography may need to be commissioned.
- **Headline character limits.** LinkedIn headline limits can change. The templates above should be verified against current LinkedIn specs before implementation.
- **About section approval.** Each GP must review and approve their bio. These are templates, not final copy. GPs may want to adjust language, add credentials, or change emphasis.
- **LinkedIn profile completeness.** Bios and banners are only two elements. Full LinkedIn optimization includes Featured section content, Experience entries, Education details, Skills endorsements, and Recommendations. These are not covered here.
- **Company page linkage.** The bios reference PhyCap but do not specify how GPs should list PhyCap in their Experience section (title, date range, description). This should be standardized.
- **Logo resolution for banners.** The confirmed logo files are 120px PNGs. Banner design at 1584x396 requires higher-resolution logo assets that are currently listed as a pending decision.
