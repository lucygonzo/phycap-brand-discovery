# PhyCap GP Content Calendar: Structure and Operating System

**Last updated:** March 31, 2026
**Purpose:** Define the monthly planning process that generates four GP-adapted content versions from a single planning document.

---

## Monthly Planning Document: The Single Source

Each month, one planning document is produced. This document is the only input the content system needs. Everything downstream (GP briefs, company page editorial, cross-posting decisions) derives from it.

### Planning Document Structure

**Section 1: Monthly Theme**
A single organizing idea that connects the month's content to PhyCap's investment thesis. Not every post will explicitly reference the theme, but the theme provides coherence.

Example themes:
- "Where clinical workflow meets venture return" (thesis-wide)
- "The evidence base for underserved markets" (women's health / health inequity pillar)
- "Software at the moment of truth" (SaMD pillar)
- "What physicians see that investors miss" (physician-led VC positioning)

**Section 2: Topic Clusters (4 to 5 per month)**
Each cluster contains:
- **Cluster name:** A descriptive label for the topic area
- **Thesis connection:** Which pillar(s) this cluster serves and how it relates to the "moment of truth" framing
- **GP assignments:** Which GPs are primary, which are secondary, and which do not post on this topic
- **Key data points:** 2 to 3 statistics, research findings, or market data that support content in this cluster
- **External hooks:** Conferences, regulatory dates, health awareness periods, earnings, or news events that create timeliness

**Section 3: Key Dates and Hooks**
A calendar of external events that create posting opportunities:
- Healthcare conferences (with GP attendance noted)
- Regulatory milestones (FDA decisions, CMS announcements)
- Health awareness months/weeks relevant to PhyCap's thesis
- Portfolio company milestones (if public)
- Fund milestones (fundraising close dates, LP meeting dates)

**Section 4: Company Page Editorial Plan**
4 to 6 company page posts for the month:
- At least 1 thesis-driven post (original)
- At least 1 amplification of a GP post (with added institutional context)
- Portfolio or fund milestone posts (as applicable)
- 1 to 2 curated shares of relevant industry content with PhyCap perspective

## GP-Adapted Versions

The planning document generates four tailored briefs, one per active GP:

### Paul Slosar Brief (3x/week = 12 to 13 posts/month)

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| Format | Topic angles with thesis framing and suggested data sources |
| Level of detail | Medium (Paul writes his own posts; the brief provides scaffolding) |
| Cluster coverage | Paul posts on 3 to 4 of the 5 monthly clusters |
| Mandatory inclusion | At least 2 posts/month explicitly referencing the investment thesis; at least 1 post/month on MHCDS or delivery science |
| Delivery | Shared at month start; Paul selects and sequences at his discretion |

### Vipul Kella Brief (2x/week = 8 to 9 posts/month)

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| Format | Ideas bank with provocative questions, data points, and suggested takes |
| Level of detail | Medium-high (more structured than Paul's because Vipul needs the spark, not the framework) |
| Cluster coverage | Vipul posts on 2 to 3 clusters, prioritizing those with clinical technology or policy angles |
| Mandatory inclusion | At least 1 post/month connecting clinical observation to investment insight; at least 1 post/month with a policy or regulatory angle |
| Delivery | Shared at month start; Vipul selects from the bank and writes in his voice |

### Robin Noble Brief (2x/week = 8 to 9 posts/month)

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| Format | Near-final drafts ready for Robin's clinical review and personalization |
| Level of detail | High (full drafted posts with evidence sourced, citations included, voice matched) |
| Cluster coverage | Robin posts on 2 to 3 clusters, prioritizing women's health, health inequity, and preventive care |
| Mandatory inclusion | At least 2 posts/month on women's health; at least 1 post/month referencing clinical evidence |
| Delivery | Drafts delivered in batches of 4 (biweekly), with 5 business days for Robin's review |

### Tim Martens Brief (1x/week = 4 to 5 posts/month)

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| Format | Light briefs with event/community context and suggested framing |
| Level of detail | Low (Tim's content is event-driven and requires flexibility) |
| Cluster coverage | Tim posts on 1 to 2 clusters, selecting topics that connect to events or community moments |
| Mandatory inclusion | At least 1 post/month tied to a specific event, conference, or community interaction |
| Delivery | Shared at month start; updated mid-month if new events arise |

## Cross-Posting Rules

### When the Company Page Amplifies GP Content

| Scenario | Company Page Action | Timing |
|----------|-------------------|--------|
| GP publishes a thesis-aligned post | Like + substantive comment (not "Great post!") | Within 2 hours |
| GP publishes a post with strong engagement (50+ reactions) | Share with institutional context added | Within 24 hours |
| GP publishes a post tied to a fund milestone | Share as an official repost with brief commentary | Same day |

### When the Company Page Posts Independently

| Content Type | Company Page Publishes | GP Action |
|-------------|----------------------|-----------|
| Fund announcement (investment, fundraise milestone) | Original post with official details | Each relevant GP shares with personal commentary, staggered over 4 to 8 hours |
| Thesis-driven analysis | Original long-form post | GPs engage in comments (not separate posts) |
| Portfolio company news | Official announcement | GP whose specialty aligns comments with clinical context |
| Curated industry content | Share with PhyCap perspective | No GP action required |

### Cross-Posting Prohibitions

- The company page never reposts a GP's content verbatim without added commentary
- GPs never publish identical content to each other (even on the same topic, the angle must differ)
- No more than 2 GPs share the same company page post on the same day
- Company page does not comment on GP posts with generic engagement ("Love this!" or emoji-only reactions)

## Investment Announcement Sequencing

When PhyCap makes a new investment, the announcement follows a precise sequence to maximize reach and maintain brand consistency.

### Sequence

**Hour 0: Company page publishes the official announcement**
- Company name, stage, check size (if public), thesis alignment
- Quote from Paul (as lead GP) or the GP who led the deal
- Tag the portfolio company and founder(s)
- Include the PhyCap thesis connection (which pillar, why this matters)

**Hour 1 to 2: Deal-lead GP publishes personal commentary**
- Why this company caught their attention from a clinical perspective
- What they saw in the product, the founder, or the market that aligned with the thesis
- A personal observation that only a physician-investor would make

**Hour 3 to 4: Paul publishes (if he was not the deal lead)**
- Thesis-level commentary on why this deal fits PhyCap's portfolio
- Broader market context
- Connection to the "moment of truth" framing

**Hour 6 to 8: One additional GP with relevant clinical context engages**
- Comment on either the company page or the deal-lead GP's post
- Add a clinical perspective from their specialty

**Day 2 to 3: Robin or Vipul publishes (if the deal connects to their territory)**
- A post that uses the new investment as evidence for a broader thesis point
- Example: "PhyCap's investment in [Company] is an example of why women's health technology is a venture-scale category..."

### Sequencing Rules

- Never announce on all channels simultaneously (staggered timing creates sustained visibility)
- The company page post is always first (establishes the official record)
- GP posts add personal and clinical dimensions; they do not repeat the company page language
- Portfolio company approval on any details shared beyond what they have made public
- If the investment is confidential, no announcement sequence occurs

## Gaps in This Section

- **Content production capacity.** The system assumes a content strategist produces the monthly planning document and Robin's ghostwritten drafts. This role is not staffed.
- **Approval workflow tool.** The process describes drafts, reviews, and approvals but does not specify a tool (Google Docs, Notion, email, Slack). Tool selection affects turnaround time and version control.
- **Holiday and vacation coverage.** The calendar structure does not account for GP vacations, clinical schedule conflicts, or holiday weeks when posting cadence should decrease.
- **Crisis and controversy protocol.** No guidance exists for pausing the content calendar during industry controversies, regulatory events, or market disruptions that make pre-planned content inappropriate.
- **Analytics integration.** The monthly planning document should be informed by the previous month's performance data. The feedback loop between measurement and planning is described in the GP network overview but not operationalized here.
- **Content bank for low-production weeks.** Evergreen content (posts that are not time-sensitive) should be pre-produced for weeks when the planning process is disrupted or GP availability is limited. No evergreen bank exists.
