# SEO Baseline Assessment

## Current Technical SEO State

### Site Structure Strengths

- **Clean URL structure:** URLs are readable and descriptive, not parameter-heavy or dynamically generated. This is a positive signal for search engine crawling and user trust.
- **Proper heading hierarchy:** Pages use H1, H2, and H3 tags in logical order. This helps search engines understand content structure and topical hierarchy.
- **Natural keyword integration:** Content on the homepage and Resources page uses terminology consistent with PhyCap's thesis (physician-led, clinical workflow, SaMD, women's health) without keyword stuffing. The language reads naturally because it reflects how the team actually talks about their work.

### Likely Ranking Positions

**Brand Terms (Likely Ranking):**
- "PhyCap Fund" - likely ranking #1 (low competition, brand-specific)
- "Physicians Capital Fund" - likely ranking #1 (brand-specific)
- "PhyCap" - may face competition from unrelated uses of the abbreviation, but likely ranking on page 1

**Competitive Terms (Likely Not Ranking):**
- "physician-led venture capital" - low search volume but high intent; PhyCap likely has limited visibility here due to domain authority and content volume constraints
- "healthcare VC" - high competition from established firms (Rock Health, LRVHealth, a16z Bio); PhyCap unlikely to rank on page 1
- "physician investor" - moderate competition; content exists but is not optimized for this term
- "SaMD investment" - niche term with low competition; PhyCap could rank with targeted content but currently does not appear to have a dedicated SaMD investment page
- "women's health VC" - growing search volume; PhyCap has thesis alignment but no dedicated landing page optimized for this term
- "physician venture fund" - very low competition; PhyCap could rank with minimal optimization effort
- "clinical precision investing" - essentially zero competition (PhyCap's own coined language); could own this term entirely

## Technical SEO Recommendations

### Immediate Actions

1. **Verify Google Search Console registration.** Confirm that the PhyCap website is registered in Google Search Console and that the sitemap is submitted. Without this, Google may not be efficiently crawling all pages.

2. **Audit meta titles and descriptions.** Each page should have a unique, keyword-informed meta title (50-60 characters) and meta description (150-160 characters). Currently:
   - Homepage meta title should include "Physician-Led Venture Capital" or "Physician Venture Fund"
   - Team page should include "Physician GPs" and specialty keywords
   - Resources articles should each have unique meta descriptions that include relevant long-tail keywords

3. **Implement schema markup.** Add Organization schema (for the PhyCap entity), Person schema (for each GP), and Article schema (for Resources content). This helps search engines understand the site's content structure and can enable rich results.

4. **Fix or build LEARN, INVEST, and PITCH pages.** Every inaccessible page creates a crawl error or dead end. These pages should be built with keyword-optimized content or redirected to appropriate existing pages.

### Short-Term Actions (90 Days)

5. **Create dedicated thesis area landing pages:**
   - /clinical-workflow-optimization (or equivalent) targeting "healthcare workflow optimization investment"
   - /samd-investing (or equivalent) targeting "SaMD investment," "SaMD venture capital," "FDA SaMD VC"
   - /womens-health-investing (or equivalent) targeting "women's health VC," "FemTech venture capital," "women's health startup funding"
   - /physician-investing (or equivalent) targeting "physician-led venture capital," "physician investor," "doctors investing in healthcare"

6. **Implement internal linking strategy.** Resources articles should link to thesis area pages, team page, and community page where relevant. Thesis area pages should link to related Resources articles. This creates topical clusters that strengthen ranking for target keywords.

7. **Add alt text to all images.** Including the Paul Slosar video thumbnail, team photos, and any event imagery. Alt text should be descriptive and include relevant keywords where natural.

### Medium-Term Actions (6 Months)

8. **Build backlink strategy.** PhyCap's current domain authority is likely low (Fund I, relatively new site). Priority backlink targets:
   - Health system and medical society websites that mention PhyCap GPs
   - Healthcare media coverage (STAT News, Becker's, Health Affairs)
   - Physician-executive program alumni directories
   - Co-investor and partner websites (FortySix Venture Capital)
   - Event coverage (Women's Health Nexus on partner and speaker websites)

9. **Publish SEO-optimized content monthly.** Each Resources article should be structured for search discovery: keyword-informed title, proper heading hierarchy, internal links, meta description, and 800-1,500 words of substantive content. Targeting Tier 3 long-tail keywords systematically.

10. **Monitor and report rankings quarterly.** Track position changes for Tier 1 and Tier 2 keywords. Use Google Search Console impressions and clicks data to identify which terms are driving organic discovery.

## Gaps in This Section

1. **No search engine ranking data available.** All ranking assessments are based on likelihood estimates, not actual position data from Google Search Console, Ahrefs, SEMrush, or similar tools. Actual ranking data would confirm or revise these estimates.
2. **Search volume estimates are approximations.** The monthly search volume figures are estimated ranges based on category norms for niche B2B terms. Actual volumes from keyword research tools may differ.
3. **Domain authority score unknown.** Without access to Ahrefs, Moz, or similar tools, PhyCap's current domain authority cannot be quantified. A low domain authority score would extend the projected timeline for competitive keyword ranking.
4. **No backlink profile analysis.** The number and quality of existing backlinks to the PhyCap site is unknown. This is a critical factor in ranking potential.
5. **Competitor SEO analysis is surface-level.** The competitive SEO landscape assessment is based on likely positioning, not actual keyword ranking data for each competitor. A proper competitive SEO analysis would use tools to identify exact ranking positions for shared target keywords.
6. **Site speed and Core Web Vitals not assessed.** Page load speed, Largest Contentful Paint, Cumulative Layout Shift, and First Input Delay are Google ranking factors that cannot be evaluated without running Lighthouse or PageSpeed Insights.
7. **International SEO not considered.** PhyCap's focus is US-based, but if any GPs or LPs are international, there may be search considerations beyond the US market.
