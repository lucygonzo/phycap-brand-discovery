## Gaps in This Section

- **Completion percentages are estimates.** Without a granular rubric defining what "100% complete" looks like for each section, these scores reflect professional judgment rather than objective measurement.
- **"Needs GP Interviews" may overstate the blocker.** Some GP input could be gathered asynchronously (via email or short questionnaire) rather than requiring formal interviews. The tracker does not distinguish between these.
- **Sections 09 (Go-to-Market) and 10 (Journey) have the lowest completion and the most dependencies.** These are the most complex sections and may require multiple rounds of input. They should be flagged as long-lead items.
- **No section accounts for legal review.** Some content (fund terms, LP journey, portfolio disclosures) may require legal approval that extends beyond the brand team's authority. This is not reflected in the completion scores.
- **Post-meeting reassessment needed.** After the April 3 Kate meeting, every section score should be updated to reflect newly confirmed decisions and any redirects.
