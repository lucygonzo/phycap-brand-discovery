## Portfolio Showcase Design Principles

### 1. Lead with Clinical Validation, Not Financial Metrics
PhyCap's differentiator is clinical depth. Portfolio showcases should emphasize the clinical problem addressed, the clinical evidence supporting the solution, and the clinical expertise PhyCap brought to the diligence process. Financial metrics (revenue, growth rate, valuation) are secondary and often confidential at seed stage.

### 2. Protect Confidentiality by Default
Seed-stage companies have legitimate confidentiality concerns. Portfolio showcases should assume maximum privacy and selectively add public information with explicit company approval. Never publish financials, customer names, pipeline details, or strategic plans without written consent from the portfolio company.

### 3. Show the PhyCap Value-Add
Each portfolio showcase should include a brief description of how PhyCap's clinical expertise contributed to the investment decision or to post-investment support. This is the proof point that backs the "Insider Precision" positioning.

### 4. Maintain Visual Consistency
All portfolio company showcases should use the same template, color system (within PhyCap's confirmed palette), and information architecture. Consistency signals professionalism and makes the portfolio page scannable as the number of companies grows.

## Clinical Validation Showcase Framework

For each portfolio company, clinical validation should be presented at the appropriate level of detail:

### Level 1: Problem Validation (Minimum)
"This company addresses [clinical problem] experienced by [clinician type] in [care setting]."
- No clinical data required
- Based on PhyCap GP clinical experience confirming the problem exists
- Appropriate for all portfolio companies at any stage

### Level 2: Solution Validation
"PhyCap's [specialty] GP evaluated the product and confirmed [specific clinical claim]."
- Requires GP evaluation documentation
- Does not require formal clinical study
- Appropriate when GP has directly assessed the product

### Level 3: Evidence Validation
"The company has [published data / completed pilot / received FDA clearance] demonstrating [clinical outcome]."
- Requires company-provided evidence
- Must be reviewed for accuracy and confidentiality
- Appropriate when clinical evidence milestones have been achieved

### Level 4: Impact Validation
"Since deployment, [measurable clinical outcome] has been observed across [number] sites."
- Requires real-world deployment data
- Most compelling for LP presentations and case studies
- Typically available only for more mature portfolio companies

## Confidentiality Protocol

### Before Publishing Any Portfolio Information

1. **Confirm company is publicly disclosed as a PhyCap investment.** Some portfolio companies may prefer to remain unannounced. Never assume disclosure is approved.

2. **Share the specific showcase content with the portfolio company for review.** The company must approve every detail that will be published, including the one-line description, clinical problem framing, and any claims about the product or market.

3. **Confirm co-investor disclosure.** Other investors in the round may have their own confidentiality preferences. Do not name co-investors without their explicit consent.

4. **Review regulatory sensitivity.** SaMD companies in pre-clearance stages may have restrictions on public claims about their products. Ensure showcase content does not make claims that conflict with regulatory requirements.

5. **Document approval.** Keep a record of company approval for all published portfolio content, including the date, the content approved, and the approving contact.
