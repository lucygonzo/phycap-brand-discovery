# Design Principles

## What the Site is Reaching For

PhyCap's website aspires to a visual register that sits at the intersection of three worlds:

1. **Medical editorial**: The authority of a peer-reviewed journal cover. Structured layouts, evidence-based content hierarchy, restrained typography, and clinical imagery that communicates credibility without sterility.

2. **Luxury membership**: The exclusivity signal of a private equity firm or premium membership club. Forest green and gold on cream is a palette that evokes private banking, country clubs, and legacy institutions. The photography style (desaturated, high-contrast, intimate) adds gravitas.

3. **Modern venture capital**: Clean digital design, responsive layout, generous whitespace, and a content-forward structure that feels current without chasing trends.

The site has largely arrived at this intersection. The visual system is consistent, the palette is disciplined, and the photography style is distinctive. Where it falls short is in the supporting assets (templates, graphics, event branding) that would extend this visual language beyond the homepage.

## Editorial and Luxury References

### Publications the Palette Approximates
- **The New England Journal of Medicine (NEJM)**: Structured layouts, serif headlines, evidence hierarchy, authoritative tone. The PhyCap website shares NEJM's approach to information architecture: clear sections, data-forward design, restrained use of color.
- **JAMA (Journal of the American Medical Association)**: Blue-green palette with warm accents, clean typography, data visualization integration. PhyCap's Forest/Gold palette occupies adjacent territory.
- **The Economist**: Dark green masthead, serif headlines, cream paper stock, gold/tan accents. The Economist's visual language communicates "informed authority" in a way PhyCap's palette naturally echoes.

### Luxury and Lifestyle References
- **Equinox**: Dark environments, selective gold/warm lighting, desaturated photography, emphasis on precision and performance. The "NEJM format meets Equinox energy" directive from Lucy's notes maps directly to PhyCap's visual target.
- **Bay Club**: Private membership, Forest green palette, gold accents, editorial photography. The membership-exclusivity-meets-wellness positioning is analogous to PhyCap's community-exclusivity-meets-clinical-authority approach.
- **J.P. Morgan Private Bank**: Forest green, gold, cream. The palette overlap is not accidental. Both brands communicate "institutional trust with a personal relationship."
- **Congressional Country Club** (where Women's Health Nexus was held): The venue itself reflects PhyCap's visual positioning: traditional authority, manicured precision, insider access.

### Architectural References
- **Clinical modernism**: The clean lines, generous whitespace, and Forest/Cream color blocking on PhyCap's site recall the aesthetic of contemporary hospital and medical campus architecture: precise, functional, and humane. Think Renzo Piano's design for the Columbia University Medical Center rather than a Silicon Valley open-plan office.

## Competitor Visual Greenspace

### Colors and Positions Unclaimed by Competitors

| Competitor | Primary Palette | Visual Feel |
|-----------|----------------|-------------|
| Scrub Capital | Teal/turquoise, white | Fresh, community-forward, inclusive. More "startup" than "institution." |
| Doctors Innovate Fund | Blue, white, tech-forward | Institutional, Alumni Ventures parent brand. Data-driven, corporate. |
| LRVHealth | Professional blue/gray | Conservative, strategic, institutional. Similar to consulting firms. |
| Catalyst Health Ventures | Clinical/technical blue | MedTech focused, technical credibility. |
| SV Health Investors | Corporate blue/green | Global, institutional, established. Corporate PE feel. |
| Rock Health | Bright teal/coral | Digital health native, research-forward, modern. |

**PhyCap's greenspace**: The Forest green + Gold + Cream palette with desaturated B&W clinical photography occupies a position no competitor has claimed. The closest visual territory is the private banking/medical editorial intersection: serious, exclusive, warm. Competitors skew either toward tech-startup freshness (Scrub Capital, Rock Health) or institutional corporate (LRVHealth, SV Health). PhyCap sits alone in the "editorial authority + membership exclusivity" quadrant.

**Protect this position by**:
- Never introducing bright or saturated accent colors (no tech-blue, no startup-teal).
- Maintaining the desaturated B&W photography style. Color photography would move PhyCap toward the competitor visual cluster.
- Using the Gold accent sparingly. Overuse dilutes its distinction.
- Keeping layouts editorial (structured, generous whitespace) rather than app-like (cards, grids, micro-interactions).

---

## Gaps in This Section

- Competitor visual analysis is based on January 2026 research. If competitors have rebranded since then, the greenspace map should be updated.
- The editorial/luxury reference list is diagnostic, not prescriptive. PhyCap should reference these aesthetics for inspiration but not replicate them. The goal is to occupy the same emotional register, not to look like The Economist or Equinox.
- Interior photography (office, event spaces, team candids) is not represented on the current site. If PhyCap plans to add behind-the-scenes content, the visual treatment for this content type should be defined (likely similar desaturation and Forest color grading as the clinical photography).
