## Logo Anatomy

Based on the Raw Discovery:
- **Primary mark**: "P/C" monogram with a stylized medical cross integrated into the "P." The cross signals healthcare without being overtly clinical. The monogram is compact and recognizable at small sizes.
- **Wordmark**: "PhyCap" rendered in the brand serif typeface. Modern, authoritative, balanced.
- **Full lockup**: "Physicians Capital Fund" as a secondary wordmark, used in formal or legal contexts.

---

## Safe Zone System

**X-unit definition**: The cap height of the "P" in the PhyCap wordmark equals one X unit.

**Minimum clear space**: 1X on all sides of the logo (including monogram, wordmark, and full lockup). No text, graphic elements, or page edges may enter the safe zone.

**Preferred clear space**: 1.5X on all sides for primary placements (homepage header, pitch deck cover, event signage).

---

## Minimum Sizes

### Digital
- **Monogram only**: 24px height minimum (social media avatar, favicon)
- **Wordmark (PhyCap)**: 80px width minimum
- **Full lockup (Physicians Capital Fund)**: 200px width minimum

### Print
- **Monogram only**: 0.375" (9.5mm) height minimum
- **Wordmark (PhyCap)**: 1" (25.4mm) width minimum
- **Full lockup (Physicians Capital Fund)**: 2.5" (63.5mm) width minimum

---

## Logo Do's and Don'ts

**Do**:
- Use the correct logo version for the background (dark logo on light backgrounds, light logo on dark backgrounds).
- Maintain the safe zone in all applications.
- Use the logo at or above minimum sizes.
- Place the logo on solid or near-solid backgrounds where contrast is sufficient.

**Do not**:
- Alter the logo colors. The logo appears in its designed colorways only.
- Stretch, rotate, skew, or distort the logo.
- Place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without a semi-transparent overlay or solid-color field behind it.
- Add effects (drop shadow, glow, bevel, outline) to the logo.
- Recreate the logo using type. Always use the official asset files.
- Use the retired Slate #404747 color in any logo application.
