# Company Overview

## Names

- **Legal name**: Physicians Capital Fund
- **Operating name**: PhyCap
- **Fund name**: Physicians Capital Fund I (2024 vintage)
- **Usage rule**: "PhyCap" is the primary brand name for all external communications, social media, and content. "Physicians Capital Fund" appears in legal contexts, formal fund documents, and regulatory filings. "PhyCap Fund" is acceptable as a secondary reference. Avoid alternating between all three within a single piece of content.

## What They Actually Do

PhyCap is an early-stage venture capital fund that invests $150,000 to $500,000 into Seed through Series A healthcare technology companies. The fund is led by a team of practicing and former physicians who apply clinical expertise to every stage of the investment process: deal sourcing, diligence, portfolio support, and post-investment strategic guidance.

The fund invests in three specific areas:
1. **Clinical Care Delivery Workflow Optimization**: Technologies that improve how care is delivered at the point of contact between physician and patient.
2. **Software as a Medical Device (SaMD)**: FDA Class I and Class II software products that function as medical devices.
3. **Women's Health and Health Inequity**: Solutions addressing historically underserved patient populations, with emphasis on women's healthcare.

The fund explicitly excludes life sciences and pharmaceuticals.

## Business Model

PhyCap operates as a standard venture capital fund structure:
- **Limited Partners (LPs)**: Primarily physicians and healthcare professionals who invest capital into the fund.
- **General Partners (GPs)**: The 10-member team led by Paul Slosar MD MHCDS, who source deals, conduct diligence, make investment decisions, and support portfolio companies.
- **Fund economics**: Standard VC model with management fees and carried interest. (Details appropriate for LP conversations, not public-facing brand content.)
- **Community layer**: The Physician-Led Innovation Circle provides a community and educational pathway that feeds the LP recruitment funnel. Tiered access: free community members receive content, events, and networking; investor members receive deal evaluation access, advisory roles, and enhanced referrals.

## The FortySix Capital Relationship

### Current State
PhyCap Fund I is co-managed with FortySix Venture Capital, a Tulsa-based venture firm. The relationship is visible in three places:
1. **PitchBook**: Lists the fund as "co-managed by FortySix Venture Capital and PhyCap Fund."
2. **Contact email**: The PhyCap website contact email is info@46.capital, not @phycapfund.com.
3. **Headquarters**: Both firms share the same Tulsa address (12 N Cheyenne Ave, Tulsa, OK 74103).

### The Brand Coherence Problem
For a fund whose core identity is "physician-led," the FortySix Capital relationship creates a perception gap:
- An LP or founder who visits phycapfund.com and sends an inquiry to info@46.capital may wonder whether PhyCap is an independent fund or a sub-brand of FortySix Capital.
- The co-management structure is standard in venture capital (many emerging managers partner with established firms for operational infrastructure). The issue is not the relationship itself but how it is presented.
- The website does not explain the relationship at all. A visitor who discovers the FortySix connection through PitchBook or the email domain has to draw their own conclusions.

### Strategic Recommendation
PhyCap should adopt a clear co-management disclosure that positions the relationship as a strength rather than leaving it as an unexplained inconsistency:

**On the website (About or Footer)**: A brief statement such as: "PhyCap Fund I is operationally co-managed with FortySix Venture Capital, a Tulsa-based venture firm. FortySix provides fund administration and operational infrastructure, allowing PhyCap's physician GPs to focus on deal sourcing, clinical diligence, and portfolio support."

**On email**: Establish info@phycapfund.com as the primary contact. If technical constraints prevent this in the short term, at minimum the Contact page should display the @phycapfund.com domain even if it forwards to the same inbox.

**On PitchBook and external profiles**: No change needed. Co-management is standard and expected in the institutional investor community.

**Priority**: This is a Phase 1 decision for Kate (before Friday April 3). The email domain issue is the most visible brand coherence gap PhyCap currently has.
