## Phase 3: May-June 2026
*Goal: Template library complete, content calendar running, GP content launch*

### May: Template Library and Content Calendar

**Week 1-2 (May 4-15): Template Library Build**
- LinkedIn post templates (5-7 formats: clinical insight, thesis commentary, portfolio update, event promotion, personal narrative, industry reaction, question/discussion)
- Substack/newsletter templates (long-form article, market analysis, GP spotlight, Women's Health category update)
- LP materials templates (one-pager, follow-up email, FAQ document)
- Event invitation and follow-up templates
- Social media graphics templates (LinkedIn image sizes, event promotion graphics)

**Week 3-4 (May 18-29): Content Calendar Development**
- Build 90-day content calendar (June through August)
- Assign content responsibilities across active GPs
- Establish content review and approval workflow
- Set up scheduling tools (LinkedIn scheduling, newsletter platform)
- Plan first Substack/newsletter edition for June launch

### June: GP Content Launch

**Week 1 (June 1-5): Soft Launch**
- First 2-3 GPs begin posting on LinkedIn using templates and calendar
- Company page begins regular posting cadence (3-4x per week)
- Internal feedback loop established (what works, what needs adjustment)

**Week 2 (June 8-12): Substack/Newsletter Launch**
- First edition published and distributed
- Cross-promotion on LinkedIn and email list
- Subscriber acquisition plan activated (LinkedIn CTAs, email signatures, event registrations)

**Week 3-4 (June 15-30): Full Activation**
- Expand to 5+ GPs posting regularly
- First monthly metrics review (LinkedIn growth, Substack subscribers, engagement rates)
- Refine content calendar based on first month of performance data
- Begin planning Q3 webinar series
- Begin planning Fall 2026 anchor event (Women's Health Nexus 2 or thesis-aligned alternative)

### Phase 3 Exit Criteria
- [ ] Template library has 15+ templates across formats
- [ ] Content calendar is built for 90 days and being followed
- [ ] 5+ GPs are posting on LinkedIn at least twice per month
- [ ] Substack/newsletter has launched with 2+ editions published
- [ ] Company LinkedIn page has grown from 1,132 to 1,500+ followers
- [ ] First metrics review completed with documented insights
- [ ] Q3 webinar series is planned with dates, topics, and GP assignments
- [ ] Fall 2026 event planning has begun

## Risk and Contingency

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Kate requests significant brand direction changes on April 3 | Phase 2 timeline shifts by 1-2 weeks | Build buffer into Phase 2 Week 1 for revision work |
| GP content adoption is slower than planned | Content launch in June is thin | Prioritize 3 most willing GPs rather than waiting for full team readiness |
| Typography or logo decisions remain open after April 3 | Visual identity deployment in April is blocked | Escalate any undecided items during the Kate meeting with clear recommendation and rationale |
| Substack platform choice delays | Newsletter launch pushed past June | Begin writing content before platform is finalized; content is portable |

## Gaps in This Section

1. **No budget attached to timeline phases.** Template design, presentation deck creation, event planning, and content tools all have costs that are not estimated here.
2. **Kate meeting agenda not finalized.** The deliverables list above is a recommendation, but the actual meeting agenda may prioritize different items.
3. **GP availability for April playbook sessions unknown.** Phase 2 Week 3 assumes GP time for playbook review and feedback, but clinical schedules may limit availability.
4. **No dependency mapping for FortySix Venture Capital involvement.** Some Phase 2 and Phase 3 deliverables may require coordination with FortySix that is not accounted for in this timeline.
5. **Content calendar topic list not pre-built.** Phase 3 includes building a 90-day content calendar, but the actual topic ideas and editorial themes are not drafted in this document.
6. **Metrics baseline for Phase 3 exit criteria needs confirmation.** The 1,500 follower target assumes a growth rate that has not been validated against comparable fund launches.
