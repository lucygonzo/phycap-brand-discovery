# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Current Stage Assessment

**Fund stage:** Fund I, actively raising, $10M target
**Deployment status:** BioReact is the only publicly confirmed portfolio company
**Brand maturity:** Early. 1,132 LinkedIn followers. No regular content cadence. No published content platform (Substack, blog, research). Visual identity confirmed (palette, archetype) but not yet deployed across all touchpoints.
**Event track record:** Women's Health Nexus (November 2025, 200+ attendees, Congressional Country Club) established proof of concept for proprietary events.
**Team readiness:** 10 GPs across 13+ specialties. GP personal brand infrastructure not yet built.

---

## Primary Growth Motions

### Motion 1: Community-Led LP Recruitment
Physician LPs are recruited through personal GP networks, Innovation Circle engagement, event attendance (Women's Health Nexus), and content-driven inbound. This is a relationship-heavy, trust-dependent motion where the GP team functions as the primary distribution channel.

### Motion 2: Inbound Founder Outreach
Healthcare founders in thesis-aligned areas (SaMD, Clinical Workflow Optimization, Women's Health) discover PhyCap through LinkedIn content, event presence, GP referral networks, and category-specific visibility. The goal is to become the first call for founders in these categories.

### Motion 3: Co-Investor Network Development
Building relationships with healthcare-focused VCs who operate at different stages or with different thesis areas. These relationships generate deal flow (syndication opportunities), LP referrals, and brand credibility through association.

## Strategy Pillars

### Pillar 1: Brand System Deployment (April 2026)
**Objective:** Establish the visual and verbal infrastructure that enables all other GTM activities.

**Actions:**
- Finalize brand system decisions with Kate (Friday April 3 meeting)
- Launch Brand HQ as the central repository for all brand assets, guidelines, and templates
- Create GP playbooks for LinkedIn content, event representation, and LP conversations
- Deploy visual identity across LinkedIn (company page, GP profiles), email signatures, and presentation decks

**Success metric:** All 10 GPs have updated LinkedIn profiles using brand-consistent assets. Brand HQ is live and accessible to the full team.

### Pillar 2: GP Content Activation (May-June 2026)
**Objective:** Turn the GP team into a distributed content engine that builds PhyCap's brand through individual physician voices.

**Actions:**
- Launch GP content calendar with 2-4 posts per month per active GP
- Develop content templates for thesis-aligned topics (SaMD regulatory landscape, clinical workflow pain points, Women's Health investment gaps)
- Begin Substack or equivalent long-form content platform
- Track engagement metrics and refine content approach based on performance

**Success metric:** 3+ GPs posting consistently. Company LinkedIn page growing at 15%+ month-over-month from current 1,132 baseline.

### Pillar 3: Event Franchise Development (Q3 2026)
**Objective:** Build on Women's Health Nexus success with a repeatable event model that generates LP leads, deal flow, and brand authority.

**Actions:**
- Plan second Women's Health Nexus or thesis-aligned event for fall 2026
- Develop webinar series (lower-commitment touchpoint for LP prospects and founders)
- Create event-to-LP pipeline tracking
- Document event playbook for replication

**Success metric:** Second major event planned with target attendance of 250+. Webinar series launched with at least 3 events completed.

### Pillar 4: LP Pipeline Systematization (Ongoing)
**Objective:** Convert GP relationship networks into a trackable, manageable LP recruitment pipeline.

**Actions:**
- Build LP prospect tracking system (CRM or structured spreadsheet)
- Develop LP-facing materials (one-pager, detailed deck, FAQ document addressing physician-specific concerns)
- Create LP onboarding process that addresses common physician investor questions (lock-up period, capital call mechanics, tax implications)
- Set quarterly LP recruitment targets per GP

**Success metric:** LP pipeline has 3x coverage of remaining Fund I target. Conversion rate from prospect to commitment is tracked and improving.

### Pillar 5: Founder Inbound Development (Q3-Q4 2026)
**Objective:** Generate consistent inbound deal flow in thesis-aligned categories.

**Actions:**
- Establish relationships with 5-10 co-investors who refer thesis-aligned deals
- Build founder-facing content that demonstrates clinical diligence value (case studies, thesis deep-dives)
- Ensure PhyCap appears in relevant founder-facing databases and directories (AngelList, Crunchbase, healthcare VC lists)
- Track and measure founder outreach sources

**Success metric:** 50%+ of evaluated deals arrive through inbound channels (referral, content, events) rather than outbound GP sourcing.

## Gaps in This Section

1. **No LP conversion data to validate pipeline assumptions.** The 3x coverage target for Pillar 4 is a standard VC fundraising benchmark, but PhyCap's actual LP conversion rate is unknown.
2. **GP content capacity not assessed individually.** The strategy assumes GPs will create content, but individual willingness, writing ability, and time availability vary. A GP-by-GP readiness assessment would sharpen Pillar 2.
3. **No budget allocation across pillars.** Brand deployment, event production, content tools, and CRM all require investment. This strategy does not include cost estimates or budget prioritization.
4. **Co-investor relationship pipeline not mapped.** Pillar 5 references 5-10 co-investor relationships, but target firms and existing relationships are not documented here.
5. **No competitive response planning.** If Scrub Capital or Doctors Innovate Fund shifts strategy toward PhyCap's positioning, this GTM plan does not include contingency adjustments.
6. **FortySix Venture Capital's role in GTM execution is undefined.** The co-management relationship likely includes some GTM responsibilities, but the division of labor is not specified.
