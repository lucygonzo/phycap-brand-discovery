# Positioning Statement

## Current Positioning Statement (to be retired)

"Physicians Capital Fund is more than just a Venture Capital Fund: it's a transformative initiative created by and for physicians and healthcare professionals, dedicated to shaping the future of healthcare. We are a connected ecosystem of successful healthcare experts including physicians, allied health professionals, operators, and executives with a shared vision to empower our investors and our portfolio companies for success."

**Lucy Notes on this statement**: "This doesn't speak to results or what you're actually building together. There's a lack of the feeling of momentum, more like a club." And: "This really isn't action oriented at all. Very vague and doesn't say much about differentiation."

Both assessments are correct. The current statement uses four words that could apply to any healthcare fund ("transformative," "shaping the future," "connected ecosystem," "empower") and says nothing about what PhyCap specifically does that no other fund can do. The statement reads as a membership brochure, not an investment thesis.

## Replacement Positioning Statement

**PhyCap is a physician-led venture capital fund that invests at the moment of truth in medicine: where care is delivered, where software meets clinical workflow, and where the most underserved patients wait longest for innovation. Our GPs bring 168+ years of direct clinical experience across 13+ specialties, including two Dartmouth-trained healthcare delivery scientists, to a diligence process built on the principle that the people who understand healthcare systems from the inside are the ones who should decide which technologies enter them.**

### Why This Works

1. **It names what PhyCap does.** "Physician-led venture capital fund" is clear and specific. No "more than just" qualifier needed.

2. **It introduces the moment of truth frame.** "Where care is delivered, where software meets clinical workflow, and where the most underserved patients wait longest for innovation" maps the three investment pillars without listing them as bullet points. It creates a spatial, active image rather than a category list.

3. **It foregrounds the differentiator.** "168+ years of direct clinical experience across 13+ specialties, including two Dartmouth-trained healthcare delivery scientists" is specific, verifiable, and impossible for competitors to replicate. Scrub Capital cannot claim MHCDS graduates. Doctors Innovate Fund cannot claim 13+ specialty coverage within its GP team.

4. **It makes a claim about who should decide.** "The people who understand healthcare systems from the inside are the ones who should decide which technologies enter them" is the fund's philosophical position stated plainly. It gives physicians a reason to invest (you should be the one deciding) and founders a reason to pitch (we are the ones who understand your market).

5. **It replaces passive language with active language.** No "shaping the future." No "ecosystem." No "empower." The fund invests, the GPs bring clinical experience, and the diligence process is built on a principle. Every verb does work.

## Positioning Statement Variants

### 50-Word Version (for pitch intros and LP conversations)
PhyCap is a physician-led venture fund investing at the moment of truth in medicine. Our GPs bring 168+ years of clinical experience across 13+ specialties to evaluate healthcare technology at the point of care. We invest in workflow optimization, software-based medical devices, and women's health innovation.

### 25-Word Version (for bios and social profiles)
PhyCap is a physician-led VC fund investing in healthcare technology at the point of care. Clinical insight, structured as capital.

### One-Line Version (for email signatures and brief mentions)
Physician-led venture capital investing at the moment of truth in medicine.

## Gaps in This Section

- The replacement positioning statement is a strategic proposal. Kate and Paul should test it in LP conversations before it becomes the canonical version. If the "moment of truth" frame does not resonate in live conversations, the statement should be revised.
- The 25-word and one-line variants compress the full statement. If compression loses the key differentiator (MHCDS, specialty breadth, the moment of truth frame), longer variants should be preferred.
- The statement does not mention the Innovation Circle, community, or educational programming. This is intentional: the positioning statement should describe what the fund is and does, not all the services surrounding it. Community language belongs in the Innovation Circle-specific messaging (see messaging-hierarchy.md).
